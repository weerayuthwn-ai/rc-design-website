How a build works

Five stages, one team

You sign one contract and deal with one project manager from the first site visit to the day you get the keys. Typical durations for a two-storey home are shown below.

  1. STAGE 01

    Site visit & brief

    We visit your land, check access, soil, and drainage, and listen to how your family lives.

    ~1 week
  2. STAGE 02

    Design & quote

    Floor plans and 3D views, then an itemised quote with materials listed by brand and grade.

    2–4 weeks
  3. STAGE 03

    Contract & permit

    Fixed-price contract with payments tied to finished stages. We prepare and submit the permit.

    2–6 weeks
  4. STAGE 04

    Construction

    Foundation, structure, roof, systems, finishes. Photos and a progress report every week.

    6–10 months
  5. STAGE 05

    Inspection & handover

    We walk the house with you, fix every item on the list, then hand over the keys and warranty.

    ~2 weeks
